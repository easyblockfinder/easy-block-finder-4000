## ❤️ Donations Are Completely Optional

- EBF4000 is free to download and use.
- No donation is required to access any feature.
- Donations do not improve mining performance or increase the probability of discovering a block.
- Mining rewards are never guaranteed.

## Security Notice

Before transferring cryptocurrency:

1. Verify the wallet address carefully.
2. Make sure you have selected the correct blockchain network.
3. Consider sending a small test transaction first.
4. Remember that cryptocurrency transactions are generally irreversible.
